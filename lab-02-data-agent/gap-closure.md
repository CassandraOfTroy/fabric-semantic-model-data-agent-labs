# Gap matrix: closed today, workaround, or roadmap

Proof-of-concept evaluations of Power BI Copilot and Fabric data agents often raise the gaps below. For each one, this matrix shows what addresses it, where you saw it in the labs, and its status on 12 October 2026.

**Legend:** ✅ available (GA) · 🧪 available in preview · 🛠️ workaround · 🗺️ on the public roadmap (planned, not committed) · ❌ gap today

Roadmap items come from the [Fabric roadmap](https://roadmap.fabric.microsoft.com/?product=conversationalanalytics), which can change.

Next: large-schema explorer in the data agent | Lab 1, step 6 · Lab 2, A2 | ✅ · 🗺️ "Explore Large Schemas in the Data Agent Schema Explorer" | [Configure your data agent](https://learn.microsoft.com/fabric/data-science/data-agent-configurations#data-agent-schema-selection) |
